Buyer checklist

Confirm it before you buy it.

Every used work truck is different. A direct conversation and an inspection matter more than polished sales language.

Ask about the truck

  • Year, make, model, mileage, engine, and drivetrain
  • Boom, crane, body, controls, hydraulics, and included equipment
  • Known mechanical issues and work already completed
  • Current photos and inspection availability

Ask about the deal

  • Current price and what is included
  • Title status and paperwork
  • Payment method and timing
  • Pickup, possible delivery, and any warranty terms
